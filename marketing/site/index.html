What you get

Every part of the week, on one screen.

Members and batches

Every student sits in a batch, with a coach and a clear standing. When a parent calls, the answer takes two seconds, not two minutes.

  • Active, lapsed and trial students, separated
  • Batches by sport, centre, coach and time
  • Joining date, plan and renewal on one row

Attendance in seconds

Coaches mark a whole batch from the court, on a phone. No paper register, and nothing left for somebody to type up later.

  • Tap a face to mark present; the batch is done in one screen
  • Coaches see only their own centres
  • Per-student attendance rate, ready when a parent asks

Fees, renewals and who owes what

This is where academies quietly lose money. See who is due and exactly what it is worth, while there is still time to collect it.

  • Collections, expenses and margin, month by month
  • Renewals roll forward automatically when a payment lands
  • Multi-centre revenue share worked out for you
